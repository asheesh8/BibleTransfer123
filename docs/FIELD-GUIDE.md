# Field guide — one card, one person, one working handoff

For whoever is carrying cards. Short on purpose; print it.

## Before you leave internet access

- [ ] `verify` every card. A card nobody tested is a card that fails in front of
      the person you gave it to.
- [ ] Open one film and one PDF on each card, on a real phone, not a laptop.
- [ ] Install **LocalSend** on your own phone. It is the one route that works
      between iPhone and Android with no account and no signal.
- [ ] Print the transfer and sharing pages from the VillageServer site.
- [ ] Charge everything.

## Handing a card over

1. **Open it with them, not for them.** Put the card in their phone and let
   them tap START-HERE.html themselves. If you do it, they have watched a trick;
   if they do it, they have learned a thing.
2. **Play one item all the way through.** Their language, something they asked
   for. This is the moment the library stops being an abstraction.
3. **Save one file to the phone.** Not the whole library — one file. Then open
   it again from Downloads with the card removed. That is the proof it is really
   theirs.
4. **Walk the Share page once.** Two taps to the steps for their exact devices.
5. **Have them share it onward while you watch.** To a third person, right now.
   Do not skip this. It is the difference between a card and a chain.
6. **Leave the printed guide.** It replaces you.

## The three failures that actually happen

**"It opens but everything is blank."**
The folders were renamed, or the copy stopped early. `app` and `media` must keep
their names exactly. Re-copy the whole card.

**"The film will not play."**
Try a second item. If nothing plays, the phone has no video player — install one
while there is still signal. If only one fails, that file did not finish copying;
`verify` will find it.

**"I saved it and now I cannot find it."**
Downloads. Always look there first. If it is not there, save it again and watch
where the phone says it is putting it.

## Things worth saying out loud

- The card works with no internet. The Wi-Fi with no internet is not broken.
- Items marked **needs internet** were never on the card — only their listing.
- A file opened from the card disappears with the card. Saved files stay.
- Nobody should ever pay for this, or for a copy of it.
