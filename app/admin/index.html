Report details

Identifiers describe consented browsers and sessions, not named people. Playback events can include resumes; a chapter ending is not evidence that an entire resource was watched. No device demographics or total watch-time metric is collected.

i

What these numbers cover

Online activity from people who accepted analytics. Visitors who decline are not counted. Offline card copies, file transfers outside the library and private app conversations can’t be tracked centrally.
