API & completeness

Report details

Identifiers describe consented browsers and sessions, not named people. Playback events can include resumes; a chapter ending is not evidence that an entire resource was watched. No device demographics or total watch-time metric is collected.

i

A clear view of what we can measure.

These counts cover online activity from people who accepted analytics. Visitors who decline are not counted. Share attempts, confirmed copy actions and visits from shared links are different signals; none identifies a recipient. The native share sheet does not confirm external delivery. Offline card copies, file transfers outside the library and private app conversations cannot be centrally tracked.
