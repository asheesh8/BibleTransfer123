Before you close the tab

Keep it open while the website is saving. With Save to Downloads, also check that the browser download has finished. A file saved directly to a chosen folder may bypass the browser’s download list.

Several selected items may create several files. Allow multiple downloads if your browser asks, and leave enough space for ZIP files and their extracted contents. EPUB files need a compatible reader.
