Helpful tips Before you save

Before you close the tab

Keep the tab open while the website is saving. Then check that your browser’s download has finished too. A file saved straight into a folder you chose may not show in the browser’s download list.

Several items may make several files. If your browser asks, allow it to download more than one file. Leave enough space for ZIP files and the files they unpack. EPUB files need an ebook reader app.

This guide saves single files. To keep a whole library from a card, copy the whole card and keep its folder names.
