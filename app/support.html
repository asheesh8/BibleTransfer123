Help and contact

EasyTransfer and The Library apps for iPhone and Android are made by the VillageServer Initiative, a non-profit that brings Bibles, teaching and films to people with little or no internet. Everything in the library is free.

Write to us

Email villageserverinitiative@gmail.com. Tell us which phone you use, whether you use the website or the app, and what happened. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

You can also visit villageservers.org.

Common questions

  • How do I share a film with a phone that has no internet? In the Android app, open the item, choose Share, then “Share with no internet” and let the other phone scan the QR code. The help page walks through every way to share.
  • Does it cost anything? No. There are no payments, subscriptions or ads.
  • Something is wrong in a translation or a film. Email us the language and the item’s name and we’ll pass it to the publisher.
